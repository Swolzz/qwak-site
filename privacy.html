Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Summary

Qwak runs on its owner's own computer and connects only to the owner's own TikTok account. It has no servers and no analytics, and it doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.

What data Qwak accesses

  • TikTok account info (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID and display name, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Upload permissions (video.upload, video.publish): used only to send videos you choose to your own TikTok account.
  • Access tokens: TikTok gives Qwak an access token and a refresh token after you sign in.

How data is stored

Tokens are saved in a local file on the computer where Qwak runs. They're never sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com) to make the requests you ask for. Qwak doesn't run any servers, databases or tracking.

What Qwak does not do

  • It doesn't read your messages, followers, likes or other videos.
  • It doesn't collect data from other TikTok users.
  • It doesn't sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • It doesn't use cookies, ads or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the local computer until it's deleted. To remove Qwak's access:

  1. In the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, and remove Qwak.
  2. Delete the local token file. After that, Qwak can't access the account.

Children

Qwak isn't meant for anyone under 18 and doesn't knowingly handle children's data.
