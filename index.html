Qwak

A personal desktop tool for posting gaming clips.

What it does

Qwak is a desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. It edits short vertical gaming clips from streams the owner has permission to use, and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in once with Login with TikTok and approves the requested permissions.
  2. Qwak stores the access token only on the owner's computer.
  3. When a clip is approved, Qwak uploads the video through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Clips go to the TikTok inbox as drafts, or are posted privately when drafts aren't available.
  4. Every clip includes edits and on-screen captions, plus a credit line naming the original creator.

Who it's for

Qwak is not a public service. It's used only by its owner, with their own TikTok account. No other users sign up or share data.
